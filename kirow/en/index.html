03 / A remarkable machine

A real crane.
A powerful inspiration.

Based at Dijon-Perrigny, the SNCF Réseau Kirow crane can lift up to 150 tonnes. This brick interpretation captures its yellow livery, rail chassis and distinctive silhouette.

A tribute to the people who look after the railway, one brick at a time.
