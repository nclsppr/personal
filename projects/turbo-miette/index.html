Game in development

Turbo Miette

A mug becomes an obstacle, the edge of a bed becomes a cliff, and the garage floor becomes a straight. In Turbo Miette I race pocket-sized cars around human-sized rooms.

On the tracks#

The race#

Pick one of eight drivers for a four-car race. Your car accelerates on its own. You have to find the right line, time your braking and save some turbo for the way out of the turn. Each round lasts three laps. The three gates have to be taken in order. Between gates, the route is yours.

In solo races you’re up against three computer drivers. You can switch between the overhead view and the cockpit mid-race. In a local duel, both players share a screen and the camera pulls back to keep both cars in view. First to win three rounds takes the cup.

In the workshop#

I’m developing the prototype in Swift. The racing rules run independently of the scenery. RealityKit handles the cars, the rooms and the lighting. A gate counts the same way whether you see it from overhead or from behind the wheel.

The game is in development for Mac, iPhone and iPad. All three screenshots come from races in the iPad simulator.
