The sites and games I build

I started these projects and decide what to build next. AI assistants help me write the code. For each release, I review the changes, test them and check the result.

I also built a railway crane out of bricks. Kirow on rails has the interactive 3D model and illustrated building guide.

Product 01 / 07

Guteneo#

With Guteneo, you can start with a PDF or a document template and prepare an email, fax or letter. An assistant can draft it for you. In the workshop, you review the document, the recipient and the price before you approve the dispatch.

StatusPublic beta. Sales on the site haven’t opened yet.

Product 02 / 07

Turbo Miette#

In Turbo Miette, toy cars weave between breakfast cups, around the foot of a bed and past the legs of a garage workbench. Pick one of eight drivers. In solo races, you can switch between an overhead view and the cockpit. Two players can race on the same screen.

StatusPlayable prototype in development for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Explore Turbo Miette →

Product 03 / 07

Surplasse#

At an independent restaurant, scanning the QR code on the table brings up the menu. Guests can order and pay on their phones. The restaurant sets its prices and handles orders directly with its customers.

StatusThe first version is open for testing. Orders and payments use test data only.

Visit Surplasse ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 04 / 07

Papers Empire#

You start with a small print shop and gradually automate production until you have a smart factory. It’s an idle game you play in your browser, which also saves your progress.

StatusFree to play. No account needed.

Play Papers Empire ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 05 / 07

Parkventory#

When someone is away from the office, they can offer their parking space to a colleague for the day. Only one colleague can book the space for a given slot.

StatusPublic beta. I still need to check the whole booking process with two members of the same company.

Visit Parkventory ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 06 / 07

Four à Nu#

Florian, Magali and I publish guides for people who make pizza at home. We look at ovens, dough mixers and accessories, and cite the sources behind our advice.

StatusThe guides are online. We have yet to publish a hands-on equipment test.

Read Four à Nu ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 07 / 07

Mon Florian#

The idea behind Mon Florian is to turn someone’s travel plans into a private itinerary they can review before booking. You can already browse an example that follows ten days in Japan.

StatusThe site and sample itinerary are online. Creating, sending and paying for a personalised itinerary are still in development.
