Product 01 / 05

Surplasse#

A direct ordering channel for independent restaurants. Guests scan a QR code, browse the menu, order and pay from their phone, while the restaurant keeps control of its pricing, orders and customer relationship.

StatusMVP in progress. Public showcase reserved for testing. Transactional production is not open.

Product 02 / 05

Papers Empire#

A free browser idle game where players turn a small print shop into a Factory 4.0. No account required, with progress saved locally.

StatusOnline, free, no account.

Play Papers Empire ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 03 / 05

Parkventory#

Parkventory simplifies parking-space sharing between colleagues. One person makes a space available and another member of the same company can reserve it, without double booking.

StatusPublic beta in production. The complete two-member journey remains to be validated.

Visit Parkventory ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 04 / 05

Four à Nu#

An independent publication about pizza ovens, accessories and mixers. It publishes sourced documentary guides to clarify buying trade-offs. No physical Four à Nu test has been published yet.

StatusPublic publication. Documentary research published.

Read Four à Nu ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Product 05 / 05

Mon Florian#

A travel-planning experience that turns a personal idea into a first private proposal to review before any booking.

StatusIn preparation. Public homepage and demo available. Real generation, sending and payment remain closed.

A deterministic ten-day Japan demo is available and intentionally noindex. It is not real generation.
