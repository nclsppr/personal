Nicolas Pieper app: support and privacy

Experiment in an engineering workshop, then explore my experience, case studies and articles in a native app. This page brings together help and privacy information for the app.

Support

Contact support

To report a problem, email nicolas@pieper.fr. Include the app version, your device model, its iOS or macOS version and the steps that reproduce the issue. Do not attach private notes or passwords.

Explore the workshop

In the Workshop tab, Under pressure offers six challenges about capacity, traffic bursts, caching, outages and budget. Adjust servers, queue size and caching, then run the simulation or advance one step at a time. You can pause and open A hint and an example. Meeting all three goals unlocks the next challenge. Replaying the same traffic lets you compare your result with the previous attempt.

Scenarios are fictional and deterministic. Credits and steps are teaching units, with no purchases or equivalence to real prices or performance. Exceeding the budget does not stop the simulation. Related case studies and articles provide context without claiming that the real systems work like the model.

Return to your workshop progress

Completed challenges, attempt counts, the last result and the best result stay on this device. An ongoing attempt remains available in its open window and pauses when you leave the workshop. Closing the window or the app loses that ongoing attempt. There is no cross-device syncing. In Settings, Reset the workshop erases its progress and ongoing attempts after confirmation, without erasing your reading notes.

Use the app offline

The workshop runs entirely on your device. Articles, case studies, projects and CVs are bundled with the app. External links and the YouTube video require a connection. Editorial content reflects the date shown in the app’s settings.

Return to your reading

The Journal lets you search for an article, save it, navigate its contents and resume at the last recorded section. Your notes and reading progress stay on this device. There is no cross-device syncing.

Manage notes

Open an article’s notebook to edit, export or delete its note. Settings also let you erase notes. An exported copy belongs to the app or recipient you chose.

Privacy

Publisher and scope

The Nicolas Pieper app is published by Nicolas Pieper. This policy describes the native app for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Last updated: September 7, 2026.

Local data

Language, appearance, saved article identifiers, notes and reading progress are kept in the app’s local storage. For the workshop, this includes challenge and finished attempt identifiers, attempt counts, completion states, scores, the last result and the best result. An ongoing attempt’s settings and state remain in memory in its open window. The operating system may include saved data in backups, depending on your settings. These data are not sent to Nicolas Pieper.

No collection by the app

The app creates no account. It includes no analytics tools, advertising SDKs or ad tracking and requests no access to your location, contacts, camera, photos or microphone. It does not sell or share local data. No application server receives your activity.

Deletion and retention

You control the retention of notes, saved articles and progress in the app. You can delete notes and remove saved articles. Settings let you reset reading progress and workshop progress separately, after confirmation. Resetting the workshop erases its results, completed challenges and ongoing attempts in all windows. System backups and exported copies are managed in their respective services. Deleting a local note or result does not recall a previously shared copy.

External links and affiliation

Links open your browser or the appropriate app. External websites, including YouTube and Amazon.fr, apply their own policies and may process data or use cookies. Affiliate links are identified before opening them. The app does not load YouTube content: the video opens only after your action.

Sharing and correspondence

Notes, articles, CVs and workshop results are shared only at your initiative through system controls. A shared result is identified as a fictional simulation. If you contact support, your message and the contact details you provide are used to reply. Your email provider applies its own rules.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy or a message sent to support, contact nicolas@pieper.fr. Changes to this policy will be published here with their date.
