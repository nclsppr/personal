Building secure platforms

I lead a team of full-stack engineers in Luxembourg. We build the tools that financial and public-sector institutions use to manage and send confidential documents.

I’ve worked in software for more than 10 years. At Worldline, I first helped build a connected home platform with more than one million devices online at once. I then worked on a banking platform that processed 10 million reports a month. When my team is weighing an architecture decision, I still like to open the code with them.

What I bring#

I started as a developer. These days I also spend time with the people who use our products and the people who run them. What they need shapes the decisions my team and I make.

  • Give engineers room to make decisions and take on more responsibility
  • Work out priorities with the business teams, taking the risks into account
  • Modernize secure platforms so they can grow while they stay in service
  • Make releases and CI/CD pipelines more reliable across products

I am most useful when a system is critical, the technical choices are not obvious and several teams need to move together.

How I work with my team#

01

Know who can take over

Every major product has an owner and a backup. When there’s a decision to make or an incident to fix, we know who to ask.

02

Decide what comes first

I keep new features, modernization, technical debt, security and incidents on one list. When something urgent comes up, we can see what has to move.

03

Let engineers make decisions

Engineers talk directly to the people who use or run their product. I give them context, ask questions and help them make a call when they need me.
